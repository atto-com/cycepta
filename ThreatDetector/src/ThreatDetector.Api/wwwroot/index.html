How to read the board

Board - blue frames. Density map of documents in UMAP space. Circles are concepts with labels underneath. A red EMERGING circle is a newly surfacing threat.
Ingest - purple. The "Fetch & Analyze" button pulls fresh RSS/CVE data and runs it through the NCD model; stream events appear under the "Live feed" tab in the right panel.
Report - green. "Generate Summaries" builds one Markdown security report across all concepts; concept details, quotes and strength history open on a circle or card click.
Controls: mouse wheel zooms, dragging the background pans, clicking a circle opens details. Press A / D to hide or show the left/right panel. The minimap in the bottom-right corner shows which part of the board is on screen; click or drag it to jump.
